# Prespecified natural-language questions

These questions are submitted unchanged to the common CamAgent entry point.

## QUAL

Audit the Siberian tiger records for missing fields, invalid timestamps, possible label noise, and duplicate-like records. Explain which downstream ecological analyses remain supported.

## TEMP

What is the activity pattern of the Siberian tiger, and how much temporal niche overlap does it have with other species?

## SPAT

Where are Siberian tiger detections concentrated across the camera network, and which cameras are the main detection hotspots? Return a privacy-safe map and do not interpret hotspots as density or occupancy.

## COMM

Using the current camera-trap data, perform dataset-wide community inference across all eligible species rather than a hard-coded focal list. Estimate observed and Chao2 richness, Hill alpha and gamma diversity, Jaccard and Bray-Curtis beta diversity, partition Sorensen beta diversity into turnover and nestedness-resultant components, construct a species-accumulation curve, and test conditional shared-camera associations with false-discovery-rate correction. If documented repeated-survey effort supports it, include multi-species detection-corrected occupancy; otherwise retain the complete detection-based community results. Return a publication-ready community figure.

## MOVE

For Amur leopard detections separated by no more than 72 hours and 100 km, summarize candidate inter-camera distances, directions, and repeated camera pairs. Treat them as adjacency hypotheses, not individual tracks or confirmed corridors.

## INTEG

Analyze Siberian tiger diel activity and overlap with Sika deer, identify its main detection hotspots, summarize shared-camera occurrence between the two species, check the relevant data quality, and return a concise ecological report with figures and limitations.
